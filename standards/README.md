# AAIP — Autonomous Agent Improvement Proposals

AAIP is DAIAA's standards-proposal track. An AAIP is a self-contained document that proposes a specific, debatable change to how autonomous agent systems produce, carry, or verify evidence — and that is ratified, rejected, or withdrawn through a recorded alliance process.

- **Process:** [aaip-process.md](aaip-process.md)
- **Template:** [templates/aaip-template.md](templates/aaip-template.md)

## Scope

The AAIP track exists to turn the proposals sketched in [§10 of the compliance guide](../docs/2026-agent-compliance-implementation-guide.md#10-standards-proposals) into documents that external standards bodies can act on. AAIPs are expected to be narrow: a single evidence format, interface, disclosure requirement, or assurance grade, not a framework.

AAIPs ratify through DAIAA. They are not ratified by, and do not bind, any other body an alliance member also participates in.

## Numbering

Numbers are assigned by the editors on acceptance of a submission, in sequence, starting from AAIP-003. **001 and 002 are already allocated** to the topics above.

This track's own process document is deliberately **unnumbered**. Some proposal systems reserve number 1 for the process that governs them; that slot is not available here, and renumbering an assigned topic to free it would break references that already exist.

## A note on naming

This track was circulated during drafting under the name **DAIP**. That name is retired. Contributor drafts and process outlines still carrying `DAIP` or `DAIP-001` predate the rename and should be read as `AAIP`; they need a renaming pass before they are cited.
